# EJPS generative-AI disclosure

**Status:** Author-confirmed on 2026-11-27. The disclosure has been inserted into the anonymous manuscript and the separate Title Page template.

## Tool and period

- Tool: ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Model version identified for this manuscript preparation: GPT-6.2
- Period covered by this disclosure: November 2026

## Actual use

ChatGPT assisted with preliminary literature discovery; comparison and summarization of cited sources and publicly available Lean repositories; organization of the research question and argument; identification of potential weaknesses; and drafting and revision of manuscript prose. This included substantive assistance with argument organization and critical assessment, not only language copy-editing.

ChatGPT was not used to generate new numerical results or Lean proofs for this manuscript. The author reviewed and edited the AI-assisted material and retains full responsibility for the accuracy of the sources and claims, the scholarly judgments, and the final manuscript.

## Policy basis and placement

The EJPS submission guidelines state that LLM use should be documented in the Methods section or, where no Methods section is available, in a suitable alternative part of the manuscript. The paper has no separate numbered Methods section, so the detailed disclosure appears after the methodological paragraph at the end of the Introduction. Springer Nature's general AI guidance additionally asks for an AI Use Declaration at the end of the paper; the concise declaration above points readers back to the Introduction for details.
