### Fit

EJPS publishes philosophical work that deepens understanding of scientific concepts and methods and explicitly welcomes constructive, critical engagement with the sciences. The present paper's focus on mathematical physics, formalized scientific models, proof verification, and scientific warrant fits this remit directly.

The journal is the official journal of the European Philosophy of Science Association. The official journal page lists coverage in Arts & Humanities Citation Index (AHCI), Science Citation Index Expanded (SCIE), Scopus, Mathematical Reviews, INSPIRE, PhilPapers, and other services. The journal page reports a 2025 Journal Impact Factor of 1.5.

### Article and formatting requirements

The current author instructions state:
- Original research articles: approximately 5,000–15,000 words.
- Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Keywords: 4–6.
- Double-blind peer review; anonymize manuscript and associated materials and provide a separate title page.
- Manuscripts may be submitted in Word or LaTeX; Springer Nature recommends its LaTeX template for mathematical content.
- Decimal heading system, no more than three levels.
- Author–date citations and APA 7 reference-list conventions; include full DOI links where available.
- References should include only works cited in the text and published or accepted for publication.
