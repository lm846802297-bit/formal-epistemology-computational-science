## 1. Primary target: European Journal for Philosophy of Science

Official journal page: https://link.springer.com/journal/13194  
Official submission guidelines: https://link.springer.com/journal/13194/submission-guidelines  
Official publication options and fees: https://link.springer.com/journal/13194/how-to-publish-with-us

### Fit

EJPS publishes philosophical work that deepens understanding of scientific concepts and methods and explicitly welcomes constructive, critical engagement with the sciences. The present paper's focus on mathematical physics, formalized scientific models, proof verification, and scientific warrant fits this remit directly.

The journal is the official journal of the European Philosophy of Science Association. The official journal page lists coverage in Arts & Humanities Citation Index (AHCI), Science Citation Index Expanded (SCIE), Scopus, Mathematical Reviews, INSPIRE, PhilPapers, and other services. The journal page reports a 2025 Journal Impact Factor of 1.5.

### Article and formatting requirements

The current author instructions state:
- Original research articles: approximately 5,000–15,000 words.
- Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Keywords: 4–6.
- Double-blind peer review; anonymize manuscript and associated materials and provide a separate title page.
- Manuscripts may be submitted in Word or LaTeX; Springer Nature recommends its LaTeX template for mathematical content.
- Decimal heading system, no more than three levels.
- Author–date citations and APA 7 reference-list conventions; include full DOI links where available.
- References should include only works cited in the text and published or accepted for publication.

The submitted manuscript has a 206-word abstract and six keywords. Its main text is approximately 7,061 words, excluding the abstract and references, within the journal's stated range. The anonymous source was formatted with the official Springer Nature LaTeX class, compiled and inspected locally, and uploaded with a separate Title Page. EJPS confirmed receipt on 2026-11-27. The exact submitted source snapshot is pinned in `submission/EJPS_SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`.

### Publication cost

EJPS is a hybrid journal. Springer Nature's official page states that authors may choose the subscription model or Gold Open Access after acceptance. The subscription route has **no APC**. The currently listed Gold Open Access APC is £2,290 / US$3,190 / €2,590, before applicable taxes. Under the no-author-fee requirement, select the **subscription publishing model** and do not select Open Choice.

No institutional agreement or APC funding is assumed.

### Generative AI disclosure

The EJPS instructions state that LLM use should be documented in the Methods section or, if no Methods section is available, in a suitable alternative part of the manuscript. The journal distinguishes this from AI-assisted copy-editing, which does not require declaration. The manuscript's substantive use of ChatGPT was disclosed in the Introduction and in an AI Use Declaration before the references. The author confirmed the wording on 2026-11-27, and the disclosure was included in the submitted anonymous manuscript and separate Title Page. The approved wording and scope are preserved in `submission/AI_USE_DECLARATION_AUTHOR_APPROVAL.md`.

## 2. Alternative journals

### Philosophy of Science — ambitious alternative

Official author instructions: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy-of-science/information/author-instructions

This is a leading specialist journal and the paper's questions about formal proof, mathematical knowledge, and scientific representation are in scope. The journal's current instructions specify an abstract of no more than 100 words and an article limit of 9,000 words for articles, excluding the reference list but including the abstract, footnotes, in-text citations, figures, tables, and appendices. The journal is hybrid; the subscription route avoids the Gold Open Access APC. The present 206-word abstract would need substantial shortening. This is an ambitious target rather than the default first choice.

### Studies in History and Philosophy of Science — alternative

Official journal page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/studies-in-history-and-philosophy-of-science

The journal is devoted to integrated work in the history, philosophy, and sociology of science. It is suitable if the manuscript is revised to emphasize the historical development and scientific practice of mathematical formalisms more strongly. Its hybrid/subscription route is a possible no-APC route; verify the current article-specific author instructions and any non-APC charges before submission.

### Journal of Mathematical Philosophy — not selected for the current draft

Official journal page: https://mathematicalphilosophy.org/mathphil/about

The journal is Diamond Open Access and has no author fees. It was founded in 2026, however, so it does not yet have an established journal citation/indexing record comparable to EJPS. Its submission policy also requires AI use to be declared and the final manuscript to be substantially human-written, and gives examples that prohibit an AI from writing a manuscript from a human-supplied schematic argument and from supplying the final abstract. Given the present AI-assisted draft, it is not the appropriate target without substantial author-led rewriting and a fresh policy check.
