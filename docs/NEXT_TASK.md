# Next Bounded Research Task

## Task

Apply the formalization-aware DDI framework to the OS reconstruction case and test each interface against the exact public theorem statements and original OS literature.

## Outputs

1. A claim-by-claim map of what OSforGFF proves, what OSreconstruction assumes and concludes, and what the Comparator audit checks.
2. A source-grounded analysis of the arity-dependent growth condition and its relation (or non-identity) to OS1 regularity.
3. An explicit table of formalization correspondence, proof validity, model instantiation, physical interpretation, and target-directed warrant obligations.
4. A short comparison with Gelfert's Hubbard–Heisenberg cross-model warrant case.
5. A revised abstract and introduction-level argument that makes no unsupported novelty claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every theorem claim is pinned to an exact repository revision and original mathematical source.
- No inference from OS1 regularity to OSLinearGrowthCondition without a proved bridge.
- No claim that OSreconstruction reaches full GNS/operator reconstruction.
- The distinction between formal derivability, model-internal claim, and target-directed claim is explicit.
- The analysis shows exactly what machine checking adds and what it leaves open.
- No new numerical computation or Lean implementation unless a specific missing theorem interface makes it necessary.

## Non-goals

No broad survey expansion; no reimplementation of existing OS proofs; no new parameter scan; no full QFT formalization; no paper submission in this phase.
