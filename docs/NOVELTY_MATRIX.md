## Provisional contribution

The revised paper's contribution is a **source-critical, case-based analysis of inter-formalization warrant**: the conditions for reusing a machine-checked result across distinct formal representations of a physical theory. The OS case distinguishes a limited positive result (Comparator's record-level audit-to-production bridge) from a cross-project composition boundary (the absence of a public instantiation of the pinned OSforGFF construction as the exact OSreconstruction input).

This is not presented as a new universal theory or technical transfer mechanism. The paper does not claim to originate theory morphisms, proof interoperability, distributed assertion transfer, semantic fidelity, DDI, mature-formalism analysis, or cross-model empirical warrant transfer.

## Novelty stress test

The contribution would be substantially weakened if prior work already analyzed the epistemic conditions for transferring theorem hypotheses and reconstructed witnesses between formal representations of mathematical physics. DeDeo and Duede (2026) already address the adjacent problem of informal/formal proof correspondence, including library lock-in and hybridization across modelling traditions; Rabe (2017), DAMF, and SC-TPTP establish relevant formal transfer mechanisms; Bobbin et al. (2024) establish interoperability in scientific formalization. The paper's remaining contribution is narrower: a source-critical account of the interface obligations between source and receiving formal records, illustrated by a record-level audit-to-production bridge and a distinct cross-project instantiation boundary. No priority claim is made.
