# EJPS Generative-AI Disclosure — Author Review Required

**Status:** Proposed wording only. The submitting author must review, correct, and approve it before submission.

## Why disclosure is required

The European Journal for Philosophy of Science instructs authors to document LLM use in the Methods section or a suitable alternative part of the manuscript. Its exception for undeclared AI-assisted copy-editing is limited to improvements to human-generated text for readability, grammar, spelling, punctuation, and tone. The present project used ChatGPT more substantially: preliminary literature discovery, source comparison, argument organization, and drafting/revision of manuscript prose. This should not be described as copy-editing alone.

## Proposed statement

> **Use of generative AI.** ChatGPT (OpenAI) was used during the preparation of this manuscript to assist with preliminary literature discovery, comparison of source materials, organization of the argument, and drafting and revision of prose. The submitting author has independently reviewed the cited literature and primary formalization sources, checked the mathematical and repository-specific claims, revised the text to reflect their own scholarly judgment, and takes responsibility for the final manuscript.

## Required author actions before adopting the statement

1. Read the complete manuscript, not only the abstract and conclusion.
2. Independently inspect the cited papers and all claims about OSforGFF, OSreconstruction, and Comparator.
3. Verify every DOI, author attribution, theorem statement, growth assumption, source commit, and interpretation.
4. Rewrite or remove any passage that does not reflect the author's own understanding and judgment.
5. Only then retain the statement above, modifying it to describe the actual use of AI tools and the checks actually completed.

Do not claim that independent verification or author approval has occurred before it has. ChatGPT is not an author and cannot take responsibility for the submitted work.
