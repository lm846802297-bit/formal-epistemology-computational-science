# In Lean We Trust? — Manuscript outline

**Subtitle:** The Ideology of Machine-Checked Proofs in Science

## Central research question

Under what conditions does the bounded technical warrant of Lean verification acquire an ideological function as a symbolic guarantee of broader mathematical or scientific certainty within knowledge production and communication?

## Central hypothesis

**The Ideological Fetishism of Formal Verification:** the epistemic authority of formal verification may be ideologically sustained when the genuine reliability of a bounded machine check is made to function as an autonomous guarantee of broader scientific certainty, while the human, conceptual, semantic, and institutional relations that make the verification meaningful recede from view.

The core theory is Žižek's critique of ideology. Marx and Lacan are theoretical sources within that framework; Hardwig is a normative comparator for warranted epistemic dependence. The Big Other is structural background, not a separate mechanism.

## Proposed structure

1. **Introduction — From a technical check to a scientific guarantee.** Define the research question, exact object, central hypothesis, and four Lean cases.
2. **What Lean verifies — the bounded technical warrant.** Explain proof terms, formal propositions, kernel checking, assumptions, and the distinct semantic and institutional bridges.
3. **Žižek's critique of ideology.** Reconstruct ideology beyond false consciousness from primary texts. Establish fantasy, fetishism, and disavowal; distinguish cynical from fetishistic disavowal; explain the structural role of the missing guarantee.
4. **The ideological fantasy of formal verification.** Explain how a reproducible local check can stage the appearance of closure of wider scientific uncertainty.
5. **The ideological fetishism of formal verification.** Explain how collective epistemic relations may appear as an autonomous property of the machine.
6. **Disavowal and the persistence of the verification guarantee.** Test when acknowledged limits coexist with continued symbolic investment. Apply the strict evidence threshold.
7. **Four Lean case studies.** Riemann hypothesis; Navier–Stokes; Erdős/AlphaProof Nexus; Liquid Tensor Experiment. Test and potentially falsify each mechanism using exact theorem artifacts, semantic correspondence, collective labour, institutional records, and public representations.
8. **Comparative analysis across the three empirical planes.** Compare explicit discourse, material-institutional procedures, and documented spontaneous-practical reliance. Do not turn this into a population-level public-trust study.
9. **Discussion — the ideological function of bounded verification.** State when the formal credential becomes a symbolic guarantee, distinguish ideological enlargement from warranted reliance, and discuss counter-cases.
10. **Conclusion.** State what the four cases establish about the ideological role of formal verification in scientific knowledge production.

## Case scope

- Primary cases: Riemann, Navier–Stokes, Erdős/AlphaProof Nexus, Liquid Tensor.
- Waring/GPT: contrast only; no Lean certificate documented.
- Coq, Isabelle, and HOL Light: brief historical context only.

## Acceptance criteria

- Fantasy, fetishism, and disavowal are reconstructed from Žižek's primary texts and kept conceptually distinct.
- The three ideological operations are distinguished from the three empirical planes.
- The proposed term “the ideological fetishism of formal verification” is identified as this paper's extension.
- Cynical and fetishistic disavowal are used only when the evidence supports them.
- Each case is checked against the exact formal artifact and the exact broader claim.
- Counter-cases and rival explanations are retained.
- The final contribution concerns the ideological function of formal verification, not a generic theory of trust in AI.
