# Stage 2 Acceptance: AlphaProof Nexus and Liquid Tensor Theoretical Synthesis

**Date:** 2026-11-28  
**Status:** completed as a documentary-philosophical synthesis; no manuscript edits made.

## 1. Question

How can the same formal verification infrastructure participate in different public arrangements of epistemic authority and subject position? Does warrant-scope transfer explain the contrast, and does LTE semantic auditability constrain it?

## 2. Accepted findings

### AlphaProof Nexus

- Credential-centred recognition is supported by the benchmark/headline framing, portable proof archive, solver attribution and variant-specific Formal Conjectures status record.
- The output archive is concentrated, while the public integration network is broader and includes human statement review, correction, attribution and approval.
- PR #4384 shows substantial review (135 issue comments and 49 review events), including correction of incomplete target scope and an m=8-only result.
- PR #3778 is a reviewed status transition for the upper-density variant of #741, not a newly solved main Erdős problem.
- The strongest candidate for warrant-scope transfer is headline/benchmark compression of formal targets and variants into a compact count of “Erdős problems”. The local status transition itself is variant-specific and reviewed, so it is not automatically a scope-transfer event.

### Liquid Tensor

- Semantic auditability is built into project practice through the Blueprint, examples, mathematical discussion, documentation and PR review.
- PR #96 explicitly states that the Ext universal delta-functor example helps establish that Ext is defined correctly.
- The selected PR review sample shows iterative explanation and review, not just kernel checking.
- This constitutes a procedural counter-practice: statement meaning and definition behaviour remain visible and operative in the project workflow.
- It does not establish that all readers inspect these materials or that external community recognition changes causally as a result.

## 3. Theoretical distinctions accepted

1. Credential circulation and institutionalized recognition can be rational; neither is itself fetishism.
2. Warrant-scope transfer occurs when public recognition allows a local proposition-relative warrant to carry authority for distinct claims about correspondence, problem closure or attribution without those additional judgments remaining equally operative.
3. Fetishistic disavowal is stronger: it requires an acknowledged constitutive gap and observable practical substitution of the credential as if the wider guarantee were complete.
4. The machine's solver-position is a public attribution, not evidence that the machine is a Lacanian subject.
5. Institutions, repositories and experts are material supports of symbolic recognition; none is literally the Big Other.
6. LTE semantic auditability constrains certificate-only attribution at the workflow/exposition level; it does not eliminate trust or symbolic investment.

## 4. Case comparison

The contrast is not opaque AI versus transparent humans. Both cases depend on Lean, human judgment, libraries and distributed expertise. The difference is the public organization of the recognition procedure: benchmark/status-centred solver attribution in AlphaProof versus a more distributed and publicly inspectable chain of source mathematics, formalization, definitions, examples, Blueprint and named contributors in LTE.

The comparison is contrastive, not a matched causal test. Task, project aim, division of labour, audience and institutional setting differ.

## 5. Claims not established

- No evidence establishes community-wide private belief that a machine independently performed all mathematical work.
- Neither case establishes universal or community-wide fetishistic disavowal.
- No causal evidence establishes that LTE transparency changes downstream community recognition.
- A public status transition is not equivalent to universal consensus.

## 6. Originality boundary

The article should not claim originality for the three-layer distinction between derivational validity, representational fidelity and significance, nor for distributed mathematical labour, human-machine collaboration or LTE as a collaborative case. Kallel and El Louadi (2026), López-Corona (2026), MacKenzie, Rosental and Ochigame are priority interlocutors.

The narrower contribution remains a Žižekian account of how a genuine local proof warrant can become a material bearer of wider public recognition and attribution through a fetish-form.

## 7. Acceptance

The two-case theoretical synthesis is complete at the level of documentary evidence and calibrated inference. The theory-evidence map has been updated. No manuscript revision has been made. Any later manuscript changes must preserve the distinction between credential circulation, warrant-scope transfer and fetishistic disavowal.
