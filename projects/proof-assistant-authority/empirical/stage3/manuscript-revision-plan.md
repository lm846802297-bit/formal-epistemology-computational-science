# Manuscript Revision Plan After Stage 3

**Status:** plan only; no manuscript file has been edited.  
**Starting point:** accepted Stage 3 evidence dossier and theory synthesis, 2026-11-28.

## 1. Revision boundary

The next manuscript phase begins with the theoretical framework only. The purpose is to align the argument with the empirical result that post-verification recognition, technical reuse, methodological uptake and theorem-level assimilation are distinct.

No fifth case is added. No new general public-trust survey is introduced. Stage 1 and Stage 2 records remain frozen unless a specific error or new evidence requires a linked amendment.

## 2. Round 1 — theoretical framework only

### Required changes

1. State the central problem as the conversion of a genuine but bounded formal warrant into a broader symbolic guarantee during post-verification circulation.
2. Retain Marxian commodity fetishism to explain how distributed mathematical labour can appear as a property of the portable proof artifact.
3. Use Lacan's Big Other and subject-supposed-to-know to explain symbolic authorization; do not identify the Big Other with a single institution or software system.
4. Use Žižek's practical belief and ideological fantasy to explain the conditional transfer from local technical validity to broader closure.
5. Introduce Bourdieu/Merton only for recognition, priority, credit and status conversion.
6. Introduce Derrida only for archival selection, versioning and repeatability.
7. Retain Hardwig and STS as the baseline for rational epistemic dependence and material knowledge production.
8. Introduce post-verification circulation as a descriptive model of credentialization, translation, integration, assimilation and archival stabilization. These are independent modes, not a universal linear sequence.

### Required distinctions

The framework must distinguish:

- kernel-level derivational validity;
- fidelity of the formal statement to the intended mathematics;
- human mathematical understanding;
- institutional recognition;
- downstream artifact or method reuse;
- theorem-level mathematical dependence.

### Prohibited overclaims

- Do not infer ideological disavowal from use of Lean, a successful build or a headline.
- Do not claim that citation, repository import or benchmark use automatically establishes mathematical assimilation.
- Do not describe the nine AlphaProof Erdős results as nine distinct original Erdős problems.
- Do not conflate the AlphaProof Erdős outputs with the separate quantum-graph result.
- Do not describe the LTE Lean 3 theorem as directly imported into Mathlib4.
- Do not infer private belief, cynical intent or population-level trust from public discourse samples.

## 3. Round 1 acceptance criteria

The theoretical-framework round is accepted when:

1. Marx–Lacan–Žižek each perform a distinct explanatory task.
2. The empirical mechanism is explicitly conditional rather than universal.
3. The five circulation modes are presented as non-linear and analytically distinct.
4. Bourdieu/Merton, Derrida, Hardwig and STS remain auxiliary.
5. The novelty boundary against Kallel & El Louadi and López-Corona is explicit.
6. No case-section, abstract or conclusion text is changed in this round.
