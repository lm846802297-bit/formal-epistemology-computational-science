## Candidate selection rule

A candidate enters the final theoretical synthesis only if:
1. a specific empirical pattern calls for explanation;
2. the concept has a precise primary-text basis;
3. its mechanism is distinguishable from neighbouring concepts;
4. an alternative explanation is compared;
5. the relevant literature is checked for prior use;
6. it adds explanatory value to this paper's focused research question.

Several theories may be combined, but each must do distinct analytical work. The paper need not become an encyclopedic survey of philosophers. An original concept is welcome if it survives the same standards.
