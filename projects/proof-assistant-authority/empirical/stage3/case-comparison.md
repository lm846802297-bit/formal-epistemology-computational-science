## Interpretive discipline

A case can receive high public recognition and little demonstrated downstream reuse, or substantial technical reuse with limited public visibility. These are different patterns. The comparison must not assume that one implies the other.

A high citation count may indicate attention, controversy or contextual mention. A formal import may be infrastructural rather than substantive. An independent replay establishes reproducibility at a specified level, not necessarily mathematical understanding or community consensus.

All conclusions remain open until the source-level evidence is coded.
