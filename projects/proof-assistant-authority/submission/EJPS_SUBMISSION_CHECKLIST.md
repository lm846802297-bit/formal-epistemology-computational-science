## Official requirements

The EJPS currently specifies:
- Original Research Paper: approximately 5,000–15,000 words.
- Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Keywords: 4–6.
- Double-blind review, with a separate title page.
- Editable Word or LaTeX source files.
- APA 7 author–date citations and alphabetized references.
- A “Statements and Declarations” section including relevant competing-interest disclosures.
- LLM use documented in the Methods section or a suitable alternative part of the manuscript.

Official instructions: https://link.springer.com/journal/13194/submission-guidelines
