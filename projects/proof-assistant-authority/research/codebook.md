# Coding guide (pilot)

- **Scope preserved:** the public claim is limited to what the cited artifact checks.
- **Shorthand:** details are omitted but the immediate context specifies the intended scope or directs readers to a sufficient qualification.
- **Scope expanded:** the wording attributes a stronger conclusion to the checking process and the cited evidence does not establish it. Record the exact missing link and check whether another document supplies it.
- **Undetermined:** source, intended scope, or supporting artifact is ambiguous. Record rival readings instead of forcing a code.

Code separately whether the claim concerns a local deduction, record translation, model instantiation, or a claim about a physical target. Record exact locator and rationale. Treat cues such as “verified” as observations, not as evidence of a mental state.
