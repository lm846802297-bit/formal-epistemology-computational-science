## Thesis

Lean’s kernel provides a genuine, bounded form of formal warrant. The article asks when the public/institutional credential “Lean-verified” acquires a wider symbolic function.

**The ideological fetishism of formal verification** is the objectal displacement through which the socially and semantically mediated authority of a machine-checked result appears to reside autonomously in the credential, allowing the warrant of a bounded formal check to stand for a wider mathematical claim.

## Three Žižekian concepts

1. **Ideological fantasy:** the local check may stage a scene in which wider uncertainty appears closed.
2. **Fetishism:** authority generated through a network of relations may appear to reside autonomously in the credential.
3. **Disavowal:** acknowledged limits may coexist with continued investment in a broader guarantee. This is secondary and conditional.

The Big Other is background, not a fourth mechanism. Lean is not the Big Other.

## Technical baseline and diagnostic requirements

Adopt Vallverdú’s warrant architecture and Kallel–El Louadi’s distinctions. Do not claim them as original.

The technical warrant is the baseline. A strong diagnosis requires:
- **S — Scope extension:** the credential is made to warrant more than the formal proposition and documented specification relation establish.
- **O — Objectal displacement:** the credential appears self-sufficient while the relations that make it meaningful are displaced.
- **E — Social efficacy:** the representation has a documented legitimating or institutional/practical effect.

The diagnostic form is S ∧ O ∧ E. Disavowal is assessed separately.

## Four cases

- Riemann: layered institutional certification and a public foundational challenge; objectal displacement not established.
- Navier–Stokes: strongest candidate for scope extension; objectal displacement and social efficacy remain unestablished.
- Erdős / AlphaProof Nexus: human validation and artifact provenance; no full fetishism diagnosis.
- Liquid Tensor: visible definition sanity checks and collective labour; counter-case.

No case is presumed to meet the full diagnostic threshold.

## Contribution

The contribution is a conditional Žižekian diagnostic account of the symbolic and legitimating function of the specific credential “Lean-verified,” not a general theory of AI trust, a new warrant taxonomy, or the first Žižekian critique of technology.
