# Response to the Anonymous Referee Report

**Manuscript:** *The Ideological Fetishism of Formal Verification*

## Overall response

We thank the referee for identifying the points at which the first draft moved too quickly from the truth of a local technical statement to an ideological diagnosis. The revision narrows the object to the credential “Lean-verified,” adopts recent component-based accounts of mathematical warrant as its technical baseline, distinguishes scope extension from objectal displacement and social efficacy, and treats disavowal as a secondary possibility. The four cases are now presented as heterogeneous comparative configurations. No case is claimed to meet the full diagnostic threshold on the current evidence.

## Major issue I — Fidelity to Žižek and the theoretical inference

### 1.1 True proposition versus ideological function

**Referee concern:** the fact that ideology is not reducible to falsehood does not establish that a true technical statement is ideological.

**Revision:** the new manuscript distinguishes factual accuracy, rhetorical compression, institutional legitimation, and ideological function. The last requires a specific objectal and legitimating relation. A correct statement about kernel checking is not ideological by itself.

### 1.2 Fantasy and the constitutive gap

**Referee concern:** fantasy risks being reduced to overgeneralization or reassurance.

**Revision:** the relevant gap is now specified as the non-identity between the formal proposition P* and the intended mathematical claim P. This is not an error in Lean and is not assumed to be unbridgeable. It is a distinct specification relation that requires its own audit. Fantasy names the scene in which the local kernel verdict appears to have closed this wider relation.

### 1.3 The object of fetishism

**Referee concern:** the first draft moved between Lean software, the proof artifact, the formalization infrastructure, and the public credential.

**Revision:** the object is now fixed as the public/institutional credential “Lean-verified.” The article analyzes how authority generated through technical, semantic, and institutional relations may appear to reside autonomously in that credential. The Marxian analogy is explicitly structural and is not treated as identical to commodity production.

### 1.4 Disavowal

**Referee concern:** the first draft treated disavowal as too central and risked inferring unconscious motives.

**Revision:** disavowal is now secondary and conditional. It requires explicit acknowledgement of the precise limitation, continued broader investment, actor-level continuity, and exclusion of contextual resolution. No case is classified as cynical disavowal.

## Major issue II — Diagnostic criteria

**Referee concern:** T–E–F–R double-counted scope extension and fetishism; technical warrant was not discriminating; reproduction was not demonstrated.

**Revision:** technical warrant is now the descriptive baseline, not an indicator of ideology. The diagnostic requirements are:
- S — scope extension;
- O — objectal displacement;
- E — social efficacy.

S and O require separate evidence. E requires a documented role in legitimation, recognition, credibility allocation, institutional action, or repeated practice. A single phrase is insufficient. The core diagnosis requires S ∧ O ∧ E. Disavowal is assessed separately.

## Major issue III — Comparative case design

**Referee concern:** the cases are heterogeneous and the first draft treated them as repeated instances of one process.

**Revision:** the unit of analysis is now a dated public or institutional claim invoking Lean verification, paired with the exact formal artifact, intended claim, specification relation, and communication context. The cases are explicitly a purposive maximum-variation set, not matched controls.

- **Riemann:** revised to include the Lean FRO/Mathlib certification, Sutherland’s verification reports, the AI-mediated exposition, and Emmerson’s non-peer-reviewed preprint challenge. The preprint is not treated as established mathematical refutation.
- **Navier–Stokes:** the m+4/m+5 and pressure-flux discrepancies are retained as specification-fidelity issues, not kernel failures or refutations of the PDE theorem. The OpenAI announcement is not misrepresented as claiming that Lean alone validates every step of the natural-language paper.
- **Erdős / AlphaProof Nexus:** the primary paper’s reported human statement validation is retained. The #26, #741(i), #741(ii), and #125 artifact/provenance findings are presented precisely and not used to claim that all results are invalid.
- **Liquid Tensor:** Topaz’s definition sanity checks and the project’s contributor record are treated as substantive counter-evidence. The case is not described as a matched control.

No case is claimed to meet S ∧ O ∧ E on the current record.

## Major issue IV — Literature and originality

**Referee concern:** the first draft omitted close 2026 work.

**Revision:** the manuscript now directly engages:
- Vallverdú (2026), whose Locus Thesis and warrant architecture provide the component-based technical baseline;
- López-Corona (2026), whose “studio mathematics” framework analyzes human-machine proof production, provenance, agency, and responsibility;
- Erfani (2026), who directly analyzes ideological functions of AI systems.

The revised originality claim is narrower. It does not claim the first Žižekian critique of technology, the first social epistemology of proof assistants, or a new taxonomy of proof warrant. It concerns the objectal and legitimating function of the specific credential “Lean-verified.”

## Revision outcome

The revised manuscript no longer claims that the four cases demonstrate a general phenomenon of ideological fetishism. It presents a narrower theoretical concept, a discriminating diagnostic architecture, and a comparative test that preserves counter-evidence. The contribution is now a conditional Žižekian account of when a real formal-verification credential may acquire an autonomous symbolic and legitimating function.
