# Žižekian case matrix: the ideological fetishism of formal verification

**Project:** *In Lean We Trust? The Ideology of Machine-Checked Proofs in Science*  
**Cut-off:** 27 November 2026  
**Status:** provisional interpretive coding under the controlling model in THEORETICAL_MODEL.md. The matrix tests the mechanisms; it does not diagnose unconscious motives.

## 1. Three ideological operations

- **Ideological fantasy:** a bounded formal check is staged as a scene of closure for wider scientific uncertainty.
- **Fetishism:** the authority generated through collective epistemic, technical, semantic, and institutional relations appears as an autonomous property of Lean.
- **Disavowal:** a broader symbolic investment may persist despite acknowledged limits. It is conditional, not a required stage of every case.

The Big Other and the absence of a final guarantor are structural background, not a fourth co-equal mechanism. Lean is not the Big Other.

## 2. Three empirical planes

Following Žižek's “The Spectre of Ideology,” each case is read across:
1. **Discursive plane:** explicit statements and representations.
2. **Material-institutional plane:** repositories, checking workflows, expert review, publication, and institutional certification.
3. **Spontaneous-practical plane:** repeated reliance on the credential as a routine warrant. This plane requires evidence of practice; it cannot be inferred from one public statement.

These planes are not additional theoretical mechanisms.

## 4. Case-specific corrections that must be preserved

### Riemann
The 26 November audit records a 1,582-page AI-mediated exposition (497-page main text, 963-page appendix, 122-page index), written by several AI models from Lean source and cross-checked. Do not describe the case as having no readable exposition. The issue is how formal checking, expert-community authorization, and AI-mediated exposition combine.

### Navier–Stokes
The m+4/m+5 inverse-estimate discrepancy and the different pressure-flux estimate concern correspondence between the formal declarations and the written proof. They do not establish a kernel failure or refute the underlying theorem.

### Erdős
For #26, the top-level Lean target differs from the prose upper-density statement, but a stronger auxiliary lemma in the same file suffices for the prose result. For #741(i) and #741(ii), the cited files/provenance are problematic while the relevant proofs exist elsewhere. Do not infer that all reported results are invalid.

### Liquid Tensor
The project explicitly made human mathematical labour, definition correctness, and readable sanity checks visible. This is not an incidental detail; it is central counter-evidence to autonomous-machine fetishism.

## 5. Comparative result

The current evidence does not support the claim that Lean is inherently fetishized or that all four cases demonstrate ideological overreach. The strongest candidate is Navier–Stokes; Riemann is ambivalent; Erdős is mixed and scope-aware; Liquid Tensor is a strong counter-case.

No case presently establishes cynical disavowal. No population-level claim about public belief is licensed by these case records.

## 6. Evidence threshold

A case supports the core diagnosis only when:
1. the exact formal object and kernel-level warrant are identified;
2. a broader claim or practice exceeds that warrant;
3. collective and semantic mediation is displaced in favour of apparent machine autonomy;
4. the representation is repeated or institutionally consequential;
5. ordinary shorthand, context, corrections, and counter-evidence do not explain the pattern.

A disavowal claim additionally requires explicit acknowledgement of the precise limitation and continued investment in a broader guarantee within a traceable actor/institutional practice.
