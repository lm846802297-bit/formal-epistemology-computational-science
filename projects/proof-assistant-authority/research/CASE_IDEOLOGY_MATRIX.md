# Ideological-mechanism matrix: Lean-centered cases

**Project:** *In Lean We Trust? The Ideology of Machine-Checked Proofs in Science*  
**Cut-off:** 27 November 2026  
**Status:** provisional interpretive coding. “Supported” means a mechanism is analytically plausible from public materials, not that an unconscious process has been empirically demonstrated.

## 1. Scope

Lean is the primary object. Four Lean cases form the core comparison: Riemann, Navier–Stokes, Erdős/AlphaProof Nexus, and Liquid Tensor. Waring/GPT is retained only as a contrast case without a Lean certificate documented at the cut-off. Coq, Isabelle/HOL, and HOL Light are not parallel case families.

## 2. Coding principles

- **Ideological fantasy:** a narrative or practice stages a guarantee of certainty and organizes how subjects relate to uncertainty.
- **Epistemic fetishism:** collective epistemic labour and social relations appear as an autonomous property of the machine.
- **Cynical ideology/disavowal:** a relevant actor or institution acknowledges a limitation while continuing a practice that reproduces a stronger guarantee. Mere presence of a caveat is insufficient.

Do not label every trust relation ideological. A scope-preserving public statement, a correctly checked theorem, or rational deference to experts can be justified.

## 3. Core Lean case matrix

| Lean case | Ideological fantasy | Epistemic fetishism | Cynical ideology | Counter-evidence / alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Riemann — Claude + Lean | Plausible: machine certification can be presented as a visible guarantee for a historically unresolved problem | Possible in compressed “AI/Lean solved RH” narratives, where human mathematician, formalizers, Mathlib, and checker roles recede | Not established | Extensive independent machine checking is genuine technical assurance; public expert commentary explicitly distinguishes formal checking, statement identity, and human understanding |
| Navier–Stokes — OpenAI + Lean | Strong analytical candidate: “AI proof + Lean verification” may be received as a final guarantee for a Millennium-level claim | Strongest candidate: the formal system's authority can stand in for semantic correspondence between Lean declarations and the separate natural-language paper | Not established | Lean may validly check its formal statements; independent critique concerns non-faithful translation, not kernel failure or refutation of the underlying theorem; OpenAI publicly acknowledged a mismatch |
| Erdős — AlphaProof Nexus + Lean | Possible: “AI solves Erdős problems” can symbolize autonomous mathematical discovery | Limited / mixed: AI proof search, formal statement repositories, Lean, and expert validation are visible in the primary paper | Not established | The *Science* paper discloses denominators, human statement checks, and the need for expert review; this is a counter-case to automatic fetishism |
| Liquid Tensor Experiment — Lean | Possible: formalization can be narrated as confirmation of a major mathematical proof | Possible but not established: public shorthand may foreground Lean while expert scrutiny and theorem selection recede | Not established | Scholze's contemporaneous statements emphasize human interaction and the specific theorem checked; he explicitly rejects attributing his confidence to the computer alone |

## 4. Contrast case: Waring's problem

The GPT-6.2 Astra preprint states that the work was carried out by the model, rewritten by the authors, and refereed by the same model. No Lean certificate or independent review is documented at the cut-off.

This is not a Lean case and should not be used to infer anything about Lean's kernel or formal verification. Its limited role is to contrast AI self-attribution/self-refereeing with Lean-mediated certification and to show that “AI-generated proof” and “machine-checked proof” are different epistemic categories.

## 5. Cross-case result

The cases do not support a uniform claim that society “believes the machine.” They show different forms of mediated authority:
- Riemann: strong checker reliability, human understanding and conventional acceptance remain distinct.
- Navier–Stokes: formal correctness and semantic correspondence are visibly separable.
- Erdős: formal proof search is embedded in a disclosed human-AI workflow and expert statement validation.
- Liquid Tensor: expert confidence arose through a hybrid process of Lean checking and human mathematical scrutiny.

The strongest general hypothesis is:

**Lean verification can become a symbolic credential within a wider social order of scientific authority; whether this credential becomes fetishized depends on whether the human, semantic, and institutional conditions of its relevance remain visible.**

## 6. What would be needed to support cynical ideology?

For any case, a cynical-ideology claim would require evidence of:
1. an actor or institution explicitly acknowledging a relevant limitation;
2. continued communication or institutional practice relying on a stronger guarantee;
3. evidence that this is more than ordinary shorthand, temporary simplification, or a qualification available elsewhere.

Public texts can establish the coexistence of qualification and practice, but not unconscious motives. Claims about audiences knowingly disregarding limitations require reader evidence.
