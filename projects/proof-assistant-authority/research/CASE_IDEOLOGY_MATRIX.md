## Diagnostic requirements

Technical warrant is the baseline. A strong diagnosis requires:
- **S — Scope extension:** credential is made to warrant more than the formal proposition and documented specification relation.
- **O — Objectal displacement:** the credential appears to contain autonomous authority while the relations that make it meaningful are displaced.
- **E — Social efficacy:** the representation has a documented role in legitimation, recognition, credibility allocation, institutional action, or repeated practice.

Fantasy explains the scene of closure. Disavowal is secondary and requires actor-level evidence.

## Case-specific notes

### Riemann
- The Mathlib statement says: for every complex s, if the formalized zeta function vanishes, s is not a trivial zero −2(n+1), and s ≠ 1, then Re(s)=1/2.
- The 21 November Lean FRO/Mathlib statement reports expert vetting, compilation, independent statement comparison, and kernel/independent checking.
- Sutherland’s shorter-proof report concerns a distinct artifact from the original 17-million-line archive.
- Emmerson’s 24 November preprint is non-peer-reviewed and challenges the foundational interpretation; its conclusion is not adopted as fact.

### Navier–Stokes
The m+4/m+5 and pressure-flux discrepancies concern correspondence between a natural-language proof and the formal declarations. They do not establish kernel failure or theorem refutation.

### Erdős
The #26 auxiliary lemma, #741(i), #741(ii), and #125 findings concern statement links and artifact provenance. They do not invalidate all reported results or contradict the *Science* paper’s reported human statement validation.

### Liquid Tensor

## Comparative conclusion

No case currently meets S ∧ O ∧ E. The empirical result is a differentiated comparison that identifies where scope extension may occur and where mediation or counter-evidence prevents a stronger diagnosis.
